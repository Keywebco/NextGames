Sovereign Run

The surveillance grid wants you logged. The Federation robot keeps running free.

Score 0Best 0
Press Space, Up, or tap to start.

How to play: Jump over the red firewalls on the ground. Duck under the purple drones that fly at head height. Space, Up, or W jumps. Hold Down or S to duck. On a phone, tap the top half of the field to jump and hold the bottom half to duck, or use the buttons. Listen: a low double beep means a firewall is coming, so jump. A high beep means a drone is coming, so duck. The run gets faster the longer you last.
