Sector Flip

Win the sectors back from the superintelligence. Every honest move turns the line.

Federation 2 Superintelligence 2 Best win 0
Press Start, or tap the board to begin.

How to play: You are the Federation (cyan). The superintelligence is red. Place a disc so that one or more straight lines of red discs sit between your new disc and another of yours. Those red discs flip to cyan. Glowing dots show legal moves. If you have no legal move, your turn passes. When neither side can move, the side with more sectors wins.

Keys: Arrow keys move the cursor. The position is spoken as row and column, with whose disc is there. Enter or Space places. L reads your legal moves. R reads the score. N starts a new game. Each move has its own sound: a rising note when you place, and ticks for each disc that flips.
